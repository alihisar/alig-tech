Health score

The score is a weighted average of the applicable checks, weighted by each check's risk level. A passing check earns full credit, a warning earns partial credit depending on the finding's severity, and a failing check earns none. A check without evidence (Unknown, Unavailable) earns none either: missing data never raises the score. Not applicable checks are left out. Read the score together with the evidence coverage; it only describes what this report looks at.

Medium: Password hashes are stored as MD5

Evidence

Rule. PG-SEC-003 (Security). Status: Warning. Evidence confidence: high (Firm evidence: a setting or catalog state).

Source. pg_authid

  • md5_rol_sayisi: 2
  • roller: yes

Interpretation

Why it matters. MD5 password hashing is weak and deprecated in PostgreSQL.

Proposal. Set password_encryption to scram-sha-256, renew role passwords in turn and switch pg_hba.conf rules to scram.

Risk of the proposal. Medium. Old client libraries may not support scram.

Rollback. The old hash cannot be restored, verify client compatibility first.

Effort. medium
